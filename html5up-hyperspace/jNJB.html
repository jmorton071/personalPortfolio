JNJB Bookkeeping Solutions

Junior year, I took Software Engineering. For this class we had the whole semester to design and build an app. My group and I decided to work on a bookkeeping software, which we called JNJB, after our intials. This was my first real experience both in software development and in team programming. It taught me a lot about the way the apps and software I use every day is made, and how much I take for granted the effort that goes into them.

JNJB allowed users to input entries into a ledger and generate financial statements based on the ledger. The functionality was limited and there was no journal, data analytics, or double entry accounting.
